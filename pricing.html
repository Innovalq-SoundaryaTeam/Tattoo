Pricing FAQ

Every tattoo differs in size, detail, placement and style — two "hand-sized" tattoos can take wildly different amounts of time depending on shading and linework. We quote an exact price after seeing your reference and discussing placement at your free consultation.

Deposits are non-refundable but fully transferable to a new date if you reschedule with at least 48 hours' notice. No-shows and late cancellations forfeit the deposit.

Yes — multi-session sleeves and large-scale work are billed per session as you go, so you're only ever paying for the day you're in the chair.

All resident artists share the same standard studio rate by style. Guest artists occasionally set their own rate, which is always disclosed before you book.

Tipping is appreciated but never required. Many clients tip 15–20% for a great experience — cash is preferred so 100% goes to your artist.
