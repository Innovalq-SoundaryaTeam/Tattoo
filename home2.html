06Before You Book

Getting Started FAQ

The questions we hear most from first-time visitors. For pricing or healing questions, see our Pricing and Aftercare pages.

Both work. Walk-ins are welcome Tuesday–Sunday for smaller pieces and simple piercings, subject to artist availability — but booking a free consultation ahead of time guarantees your slot, especially for custom or larger work.

You must be 18 or older for a tattoo, no exceptions. Most piercings are available from 16 with a parent or guardian present to sign consent; a few placements are 18+ only. Bring valid photo ID either way.

Reference images or a rough idea of the design, a sense of placement and size, and photo ID. If you're covering up or working around an existing tattoo, bring a clear photo of that area too.

Small tattoos and most piercings usually take under an hour. Custom and larger pieces are booked in multi-hour sessions, and your artist will map out a realistic timeline with you at the consultation.

Yes — every tattoo includes one free touch-up within 60 days of your session, once it's fully healed. Just book a touch-up slot through your client dashboard or give us a call.
